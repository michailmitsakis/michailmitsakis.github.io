  1. ChemRxiv
    Bayesian Optimization Hackathon for Chemistry and Materials
    Sterling Baird, Mehrad Ansari, Zartashia Afzal, and 122 more authors
    ChemRxiv, Jun 2025
    Abs DOI Bib HTML Code

    The Acceleration Consortium and Merck KGaA hosted a 2-day virtual hackathon on March 27-28, 2024, bringing together scientists to explore, collaborate, and innovate in the field of Bayesian optimization for the physical sciences. Participants were encouraged to select or develop Bayesian optimization algorithms, apply them to benchmarking tasks, design new benchmarks, create instructional tutorials, and describe real-world applications. With over 100 participants across 69 academic, industry, and government organizations located in 59 cities, 19 countries, and 4 continents, this was a global event.
